How Lifeline handles your data

Plain-language summary of what is stored and who can see it.

What we store

  • Your email address and a scrambled (hashed) version of your password. We can't see your password.
  • The events, eras and photos you add to your timeline, including anything you import from a spreadsheet or Facebook.

Facebook and Instagram downloads

When you import a Facebook or Instagram download, the file is opened in your own browser. Only the posts you choose to add, and their photos, are saved to your account. Your import progress is kept in your browser so you can finish later.

If you use "Find life events with Claude", the text of your posts (never photos) is sent to Anthropic's Claude service to suggest which posts are life events. Under Anthropic's commercial terms, text sent through its API isn't used to train its models.

Who can see your timeline

Only you, while you're signed in. If you create a share link, anyone who has that link can view that one timeline, its eras and photos, but not change them or see your other timelines. You can turn a link off or delete it at any time.

When you open someone's share link

The timeline's owner can see that their link was opened, when, how many times, and a general description of the device (for example "iPhone · Safari"). To tell repeat visits apart, Lifeline sets a cookie in your browser with a random code. Lifeline doesn't record your IP address, location or name, and the owner can't see anything else about you.

Deleting your data

You can clear your timeline, or delete your whole account, from the Share & account tab. Deleting your account permanently removes your email, timeline and photos, and stops any share links from working.

Cookies

Lifeline uses one cookie to keep you signed in, and one with a random code when you open a share link, so the owner sees repeat visits as one person. There's no advertising and no tracking across other websites.
